Assignment Help Tips

Many assignment questions are hard to resolve well by email. Helping you effectively means seeing what you have done and hearing how you are thinking about the problem, which usually takes far less time in person or over a screen share than it does in writing back and forth.

How to Get Help

  • Come to office hours, in person or virtually over Teams. Screen sharing lets us review your work together, and it is usually the fastest way to resolve a question. No advance notice is needed.
  • If you need a time outside office hours, request an appointment in advance. If you send materials ahead of a meeting, include all relevant files and a short description of what you would like to discuss.
  • Bring your work. Show what you have tried and where you are stuck. This helps us get to the issue quickly and shows you have done the preliminary work.
  • Be ready to explain your steps in your own words, including where you think the problem starts. I will usually begin by asking you to walk me through what you have done.
  • Expect guidance, not a fix. My role is to help you understand the issue and point you toward the resources to solve it, not to complete the work for you. Some problems take more than one meeting to fully resolve.
  • Start early. My availability is listed in the syllabus. Weekend due dates give you more time to work, not a guarantee that email will be answered over the weekend.

Reaching out early, with your own work in hand, is the fastest path to getting unstuck.
